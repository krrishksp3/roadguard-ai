## 🎯 DEMO 1: Core Web Lifecycle Demo (Citizen → AI → Authority → Verification)

### Step 1: Open the Application
- Open `http://localhost:5173` in your browser.
- Point out the clean civic-tech design, problem positioning (**"From Road Complaints to Intelligent, Accountable Road Action"**), and the 4-step workflow banner.

### Step 2: Citizen Incident Report
- Click **"Report Issue"** in the top navigation or the main hero button.
- Select the **"Deep Asphalt Pothole"** preset (or enter an image URL).
- Notice the auto-populated GPS coordinates (`28.9950, 77.7120`) and the interactive Leaflet map pin.
- Click on the map to show judges that citizens can fine-tune the exact hazard pin location.
- Click **"Submit Road Report"**.

### Step 3: Automated AI Analysis & Dynamic Risk Evaluation
- The screen redirects immediately to the complaint details view (e.g. `RG-MRT-2026-XXXXXX`).
- Show judges the **Dynamic Road Risk Score (e.g. 66–88/100)**:
  - Explain the 6 weighted factors: Defect Severity (25%), Safety Skid Hazard (20%), Corridor Traffic Importance (20%), Cluster Density (15%), Chronic Recurrence (10%), and SLA Urgency (10%).
  - Highlight: *"The authority can clearly see WHY this score is high."*
- Show the **AI Computer Vision Diagnosis**:
  - Pothole detection, confidence score, and technical civil remediation recommendation.
- Show the **Responsible Authority**:
  - Automatically mapped to **UP PWD Meerut (Provincial Division)** based on GPS coordinates without citizen manual selection.
- Show the **Linked Public Tender Card**:
  - Official Tender Ref `1102/24A/2024-25` from UP e-Procurement.
  - Work description, sanctioned value, contractor name, and verified public record link.

### Step 4: E-Commerce Delivery Tracking Timeline
- Scroll down to the delivery-style progress tracker:
  - `Report Created` (Checked)
  - `AI Analyzed` (Checked)
  - `Department Assigned` (Checked)
  - `Acknowledged` (Pending)
  - `Inspection -> Repair -> AI Verified -> Resolved`

### Step 5: Authority Operations Dashboard
- Click **"Authority Dashboard"** in the navigation bar.
- Point out the executive KPI metrics:
  - **Total Reports** (33+)
  - **Critical Hazards** (Risk Score >= 80)
  - **SLA Overdue** cases
  - **Resolved** cases
- Show the **Geospatial Incident Map**:
  - 30+ color-coded pins across Meerut corridors.
- In the **Risk-Ranked Worklist**, search or locate the newly submitted complaint.
- Click **"Manage"**.

### Step 6: Authority Status Update & Remediation
- On the complaint management card, click **"Acknowledge"** to start the official SLA clock.
- Notice the delivery timeline updates to `Complaint Acknowledged`.

### Step 7: AI-Assisted Repair Verification & Closure
- Scroll to the **"AI-Assisted Repair Verification & Audit"** section.
- Click **"Load Demo Fixed Evidence"** (or upload contractor's after-repair image).
- Click **"Run AI-Assisted Repair Audit"**:
  - Observe the Computer Vision evaluation:
    - **Location Match Confidence**: `94%`
    - **Visible Improvement Score**: `89%`
    - **Remaining Damage**: `12%`
    - **Recommendation**: `PASS`
  - Emphasize to the judges: *"AI is decision support. Final closure remains under authorized human control."*
- Click **"Approve & Formally Close"**.
- The status instantly changes to **`RESOLVED`**!
